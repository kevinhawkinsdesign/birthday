✦  A note from Kevin  ✦

To my favourite people,

Turning 32 feels like the right moment to do things differently. No big party, no grand gesture — just a small group of people I love, in the mountains, with good wine and open sky.

You're here because you matter to me. I'd rather spend my birthday around a fire with a handful of real friends than in a room full of noise — with the people I care about just down the hall, out on the terrace, or somewhere in the hills.

It'll be quiet and full — of nature, long meals, maybe a solar eclipse, and definitely wine. I just want to be with you. That's the whole plan.

With so much love,

The Plan

A week of everything

We'll be based at Mas el Vilaró with days full of good food, open air, and whatever mood strikes.

🥾

Hiking

The Osona countryside is right on our doorstep — trails through oak and beech forest, quiet valleys, and sweeping views. All levels welcome.

🦌

Hunting

The hills around the mas are classic hunting country. Whether you're an old hand or a first-timer, we'll have days out in the field to look forward to.

🏊

Swimming

Cool off in the pool at the mas — cold drinks, good conversation, and zero agenda. The height of an August afternoon in Catalonia.

🍷

Wine Tastings

Two exceptional bodegas are right on our doorstep. Celler Sant Miquel d'Oló — a family winery at 800m with a Romanesque hermitage and tastings of 4 wines with local products (Saturday only). And Les Acàcies in Avinyó — claimed to be the smallest winery in the world, guided vineyard tour with cheese, sausages and pa amb tomàquet for €15/person.

⛳

Golf

Club de Golf Montanyà is a scenic 18-hole course designed by David Thomas, set in the hills with stunning mountain backdrops. 9 holes €100 · 18 holes €140.

🌑

Total Solar Eclipse

On August 12, a total solar eclipse crosses Spain at sunset. Drive ~2 hrs to Tarragona for totality, or watch a stunning deep partial eclipse from the mas. One of nature's greatest spectacles — and it's happening right on our doorstep.

🏺

Pottery

Get your hands in the clay. A grounding, unhurried activity that fits perfectly with the pace of the week — and you leave with something you actually made.

⭐

Disfrutar, Barcelona

⭐⭐⭐ Three Michelin stars. Ranked #1 restaurant in the world for 2024 by The World's 50 Best Restaurants. Private dinner for 2–4 people (€290–325/person). My concierge Sonia can arrange tables with a month's notice. An evening you won't forget.

🪵

Just Chilling

Long dinners on the terrace. Evening fires. Slow mornings with coffee and nowhere to be. The best kind of week.

Getting There

Travel Guide

Fly into Barcelona, then take the train up to Vic and grab a taxi to the mas — or rent a car for total freedom. One of the easier trips in Europe.

FlyBarcelona El Prat (BCN)

Barcelona El Prat Airport — main international hub, roughly 75km south of the venue.

Aim to land August 11 to collect rental car and reach hotel before evening.

Departing August 16? Allow 2+ hours to the airport.

Book early — August is peak season.

Train + TaxiTrain to Vic, then a Taxi

🚆  Easiest without a car: the R3 train to Vic, then a short taxi to the mas.

Train (R3): Rodalies R3 from Barcelona (Sants or Plaça Catalunya) toward Vic/Ripoll — roughly 1h20 to Vic station.

Taxi from Vic: ~15–20 min from the station to Mas el Vilaró. Best to call ahead — cars aren't always waiting.

Prefer to drive? Rent a car at BCN (~1 hr via the C-17 north). GPS: Mas el Vilaró, BV-4601, 08503 Sant Bartomeu del Grau.

KnowWeather & Essentials

Weather: Hill country. August 24–30°C days, cool evenings. Bring a light layer.

Visa: EU/EEA free entry. Others check Spain requirements.

Currency: Euro (€). Cards widely accepted. Bring some cash.

Data: Consider EU eSIM (Airalo). Language: Catalan/Spanish. English fine in most places.
