What to see

Vitosha MountainВитоша · the city's mountain

A whole mountain on the doorstep. Ride the cable car up, hike to Cherni Vrah (2290m), find the Boyana waterfall and the surreal "Golden Bridges" stone river — with sunset views over the whole city. More →

Trails €0 · cable car ~€8

Seven Rila LakesСедемте езера · glacial lakes

Bulgaria's most jaw-dropping day trip — a chairlift and a hike up to seven mirror-still glacial lakes across the Rila massif. Bring good shoes and a camera. More →

Chairlift ~€10 · day tour ~€40–60

Traditions · обичаи (customs)

Know before you go

A little folklore goes a long way — here's what makes Bulgaria, Bulgaria.

1🧵

Martenitsaмартеница · red & white threads

Each spring Bulgarians wear red-and-white yarn for health, then tie it to a blossoming tree when they spot the first stork. It's why our whole theme is red & white.

2🥂

Nazdrave!наздраве · cheers

Toasting is serious — lock eyes, clink, and never toast with an empty glass. Rakia (fruit brandy) comes first, sipped slowly with a shopska salad.

3🔄

Yes means noда / не · the head-shake

Bulgaria's famous quirk: a nod can mean "no" and a head-shake means "yes." When in doubt, say it out loud — да (yes) or не (no).

Horo — хоро (the folk dance) · a circle dance; join hands and follow the person on your right. everyone dances
Name days — имен ден (name day) · share a saint's name and you're celebrated like it's your birthday. no invite needed
Banitsa & luck — баница (cheese pastry) · the New Year one hides fortunes and a lucky coin. find the coin
Guest is king — гостоприемство (hospitality) · expect to be over-fed and out-toasted. Bring a small gift; shoes off indoors. say да to seconds

Come hungry

Some of the best value eating in Europe — big plates, cold rakia, and a €15 feast.

🥗🥗

The classicsшопска салата · shopska salad

Shopska salad, banitsa, tarator, grilled kebapche & kavarma. A proper mehana (tavern) dinner with drinks is hard to beat. Top restaurants →

Mehana dinner €12–20pp · street snack €1–2

Rakia & wineназдраве · cheers

Bulgaria's fruit brandy is the national toast — sip it slow, three-deep. The local wine (Melnik, Mavrud) is excellent and low-priced.

Rakia shot ~€2 · wine €4–6/glass

Getting there · пътуване (travel)

Flights to Sofia

Sofia Airport (SOF) is well connected, and Bulgaria is one of the lowest-cost places to fly into in Europe.

✈🇬🇧

From the UK

Direct on Wizz Air & Ryanair from London, plus regional airports. ~3h. Book ahead for the lowest fares.

≈ €45–140 return
✈🇪🇺

From Europe

Direct from most major hubs — Vienna, Berlin, Paris, Milan, Istanbul. 2–3h and frequent.

≈ €50–160 return
✈🌍

From further

One easy connection via a European or Istanbul hub. From the US, count on ~10–13h total.

≈ €600–880 return

Compare fares on Skyscanner. From SOF it's ~20 min into the centre — metro ~€0.80 or a taxi ~€8–12. Bulgaria adopted the euro (€) in 2026, though some prices may still show лв (lev).
