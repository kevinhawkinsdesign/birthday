The City · градът

Why Sofia

Roman ruins under glass, golden domes, a mountain at the end of the metro line — and three things you can't miss.

1 ⛰️

Vitoshaplanината над града

The mountain rising straight out of the city. Hikes, cable cars, and the best views back over Sofia.

2 ⛪

Old TownАлександър Невски

The gold-domed Alexander Nevsky cathedral, Roman Serdica beneath your feet, and cobbled cafés everywhere.

3 🥂

Food & Rakiaшопска салата

Shopska salad, banitsa, grilled everything, and rakia toasts that go three-deep. Come hungry.

Getting There · пътуване

How to reach Sofia

✈ ✈️

Fly to SOF

Sofia Airport (SOF) has direct flights from most of Europe. From there it's ~20 min into the centre by metro or taxi.

🚇 🚇

Getting around

Central Sofia is walkable, the metro is cheap and easy, and taxis (yellow, metered) are inexpensive. Vitosha is at the end of the line.

лв 💶

Good to know

Currency is the lev (лв). Language is Bulgarian (Cyrillic — nod for "yes", shake for "no"!). EU/most travellers enter visa-free.
